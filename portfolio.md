## Institutional Leadership & Systems Architecture (Consolata School, Nairobi)

### 1. Automated Exit Clearance System (AECS)
*Role: Lead Systems Architect & Deputy Principal (Academics)* | *[Proposal & Briefing Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1y0PSWrZ1Hegx5d2UGLDhzmPItfrY2gBUydi21VbMONw/edit)*
* **6-Stage Sequential Routing Engine:** Engineered a 100% paperless clearance Web App hosted on Google Workspace (`clearance@consolataschool.co.ke`) that sequentially routes student exit requests through six approval stages: *Class Teacher ➔ Laboratories ➔ Sports/PE ➔ Library ➔ Accounts/Finance ➔ Section Head*.
* **Parent & Staff Portals:** Designed a parent-facing initiation portal using student Admission Numbers and a 1-click mobile/desktop approval interface for departmental reviewers with direct unreturned item tracking.
* **Automated Document & Administrative Triggers:** Built an automated PDF creation engine generating official signed certificates on Consolata letterhead (`Consolata_Clearance_Certificate_StudentName_AdmNo.pdf`), triggering automatic LMS roster deactivation prompts, and sending final clearance notifications to the Principal.

### 2. Digital Behaviour Engineering: The "GlassBox" System
*Role: Co-Project Lead & Technical Architect*
* **System Architecture:** Designed and deployed **"GlassBox"**, a custom Google Workspace-based behavior management infrastructure operating on a two-tier data governance model (Heads of Sections as "Data Gatekeepers" validating inputs; classroom teachers as "Data Contributors" logging observations).
* **Tri-Framework Alignment:** Directly maps real-time student actions against three core benchmarks: Consolata School Values, Kenyan CBE Core Competencies, and Cambridge Learner Attributes.
* **Quantitative Operating Baseline (3-Month Operating Baseline across 500+ Students):**
  * **94 active teachers** contributing daily data seamlessly across 7 school sections.
  * **4,152 total observations** logged.
  * **42,198 positive "Poa" points** awarded vs. **-6,730 "Doa" infraction points**, maintaining a **4.34 positive-to-negative ratio** that reinforces positive character development.
  * **50 severe incidents** isolated, tracked, and remediated early.
* **Institutional Recognition & International Impact:** Key operational driver behind Consolata School winning the **Leadership Innovation Award (2026)** at the Great Place to School Awards (organized by Global Peace Foundation, Kenya). Selected for international academic benchmarking and exchange at **Heyden, China**.
